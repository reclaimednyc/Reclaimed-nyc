Our Story

Reclaim Deco started in July 2026 with two friends and one couch.

Eric Reid and Charlie Hodge met at the dog park and became fast friends. Eric was finishing his MBA at CU Boulder. Charlie had just finished his degree in media and film at VCU in Richmond, Virginia. Neither of us knew exactly what came next.

Then a family member needed to sell a couch. We sold it. Then another, and another. Soon we had a 1,000-square-foot warehouse in Jersey City, and before long we’d outgrown it as we bought more inventory and served more customers. Today we work out of a 5,000-square-foot warehouse in Matawan, New Jersey.

Every day is still a learning process. We’re obsessed with great design and with making buying furniture easy, from your first message to the day it’s in your home. We like what we do, and we’re glad you’re here.

Sustainability

Furniture waste is a big problem, and no single company solves it, us included. Here’s the picture:

  • 12.1 million tons. That’s how much furniture Americans threw out in 2018, up from 2.2 million tons in 1960. About 80% went to landfill, and less than 1% was recycled.
  • Fast furniture works like fast fashion. It’s built cheap, styled for the moment and made to be replaced, often lasting just five to seven years. A well-made piece can last decades.
  • It’s hard to undo. Much of it is particleboard and MDF bonded with formaldehyde-based glues, which is tough to repair or recycle. Some foam has also been treated with flame-retardant chemicals. Many pieces are shipped thousands of miles from overseas factories, only to end up in a landfill close to home.

Wherever you shop, a few things help: look at what a piece is made of, buy secondhand when you can, and buy better quality, less often.

That’s why we sell well-made furniture that already exists, and restore it when it needs it.

How to Order
  1. Find a piece. Browse the shop. Every listing shows the price, condition and photos.
  2. Message us. Tap Message Reclaim Deco on any piece to reach the owners directly on Facebook Messenger, or email eric@reclaimed.nyc.
  3. Confirm the details. We confirm availability, answer questions on dimensions and condition, and walk you through the purchase.
  4. Delivery. We quote delivery based on distance and schedule a time that works for you.
  5. Want to see it first? Visit our Matawan, NJ warehouse by appointment.

Don’t see what you’re looking for? Send us a request. We’ll search our network of suppliers and try to find it for you.

Sell / Consign

Have a piece you’d like to sell? We’re happy to hear about it.

What we look for: furniture in like-new or excellent condition, from the brands we carry or of the same quality.

Two ways to work with us:

  • Cash offer. We buy the piece outright.
  • Consignment. We list it on our platform and handle the sale for you.

Send us a few photos, the brand and model, and where the piece is located. Not every piece will be a fit, but we’ll always get back to you.

Delivery

Local delivery. We deliver across the New York metro area, including New Jersey, New York City and the surrounding region. Pricing is on a sliding scale based on mileage. Message us for a quote.

Nationwide. We ship anywhere in the U.S. through uShip. Tell us where it’s going and we’ll submit your piece for a quote.

We only charge what it costs to get your piece to your door, and we keep that as low as we can so the savings go to you.
